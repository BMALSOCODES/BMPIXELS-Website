Cookie Policy

Last Updated: July 31, 2026

BM Pixels values your privacy and strives to provide a seamless online experience. This Cookie Policy outlines how we use cookies and similar technologies on our website, their purpose and your options for managing them.

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device (e.g., computer, tablet, or smartphone) when you visit a website. They enable us to recognize your device, store preferences and enhance functionality. Cookies can be "session cookies" (temporary, deleted when you close your browser) or "persistent cookies" (stored until they expire or are manually deleted).

2. Why We Use Cookies

  • Essential Functionality: Ensure the website operates correctly, such as enabling navigation and secure access.
  • Performance Analysis: Gather anonymous data on how visitors interact with our site to improve its efficiency and user experience.
  • Personalization: Remember your preferences (e.g., language settings) for a tailored experience.
  • Analytics: Track usage patterns (e.g., via Google Analytics) to optimize content and design, typically anonymously unless you consent otherwise.
  • Marketing: Deliver relevant advertisements based on your browsing behavior, only with your explicit consent.

4. Your Control Over Cookies

You have several options to manage cookies: via our consent popup (accept all or decline non-essential), browser settings to block/delete cookies, or third-party opt-out tools (e.g., Google Analytics). Blocking essential cookies may impair site functionality.

5. Third-Party Cookies

We may integrate third-party services (e.g., Google Analytics, social media plugins) that set their own cookies. These providers maintain separate privacy policies, which we recommend reviewing.
