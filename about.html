About BM Pixels

We are passionate storytellers capturing life's most beautiful moments through the lens.

Founded in 2024, BM Pixels is a premier photography & videography studio dedicated to delivering exceptional visual experiences. Our mission is to blend creativity with technical expertise, offering personalized services that range from intimate portraits to grand events. Based in Nairobi, we’ve built a reputation for excellence, working with clients across Kenya and beyond.

We create timeless images that resonate with emotion and artistry.

We specialize in photography that feels alive, from high-intensity moto and sports moments to the raw beauty of safari landscapes. Whether it’s speed, power, or nature in its purest form, every frame is made to hit different. But it doesn’t stop at the camera. BM Pixels also delivers bold graphic design: posters, logos and creative branding that stand out. With a developer background in the mix, every project is built with both style and structure. Photography with energy. Design with intention. Creativity that actually moves.
